# Shadow Scribe — Brand Foundation

---

## Name

**Shadow Scribe** carries two ideas in tension:

- **Shadow** — the presence that follows alongside, unseen; the understudy; the nocturnal; something that exists because of light
- **Scribe** — the one who records; the keeper of meaning; the ancient office of preservation

Together: a quiet recorder that accompanies the reader through a book's shadow — the experience of reading it — and preserves what matters.

The name should never feel like a startup brand or a product name. It should feel like a guild name, a role, a vocation.

## Voice

### Tone Registers

| Register | When used | Example |
|----------|-----------|---------|
| Observational | Companion Insights, Momentum | "You've covered a third of the story's ground." |
| Inviting | Empty states, CTAs | "Every story withholds something." |
| Archival | Dates, completion, print view | "The story ended here — May 8, 2026." |
| Gentle | Warnings, confirmations | "Move this companion to the archive? It will remain there, unchanged, until you return." |
| Literary | Chapter labels, milestones | "The chronicle begins when you do." |

### Voice Qualities

The voice is:
- **Unhurried** — never terse, never pinging for attention
- **Earned** — observations feel like they were paid for by the reader's time with the book
- **Specific** — "fourteen chapters" not "a while"; "the quiet space between chapters" not "reading break"
- **Non-prescriptive** — never tells the reader how to feel or what to do
- **Warm but not effusive** — never congratulates, never cheers

### Voice Anti-Patterns

❌ "You're on a 5-day streak! Keep it up!"  
✅ "Five days running."

❌ "Add your first character!"  
✅ "The companion will hold whoever you introduce to it."

❌ "Update progress"  
✅ "Tell the companion where you are"

❌ "Done" / "Close"  
✅ "Return to the companion ✦"

❌ "Note added successfully."  
✅ *(silent — the note is simply there)*

## Personality Attributes

If Shadow Scribe were a person, they would be:

- The friend who has read more than anyone you know, but never makes you feel like less for it
- The librarian who remembers what you borrowed three years ago and asks how it went
- The reader who annotates carefully in pencil, not pen
- Curious. Patient. Precise.

They would not be:
- The algorithm recommending what you read next
- The app sending you push notifications
- The dashboard showing you your reading statistics
- The social platform where your reading is public

---

## The ✦ Glyph

The `✦` (Black Four-Pointed Star, U+2736) appears at moments of quiet completion:

- "Return to the companion ✦"
- "archived ✦"
- "✦ Begin the companion"

It is used with restraint — maximum two or three places in the whole UI. It marks moments of arrival, not milestones of achievement. It should feel like putting down a pen.
