# Shadow Scribe — Product Positioning

---

## The Category

Shadow Scribe occupies a category that doesn't yet have a clean name: **personal reading intelligence**. It is not a book tracker (Goodreads), not a highlights manager (Readwise), not a reading journal (paper), and not a book club tool. It is the layer of interpretation and memory between the reader and the book.

---

## The Reader We're Building For

**Primary reader:**
- Reads seriously — at least one book per month, often more
- Annotates, underlines, or dog-ears
- Has strong feelings about books they've finished
- Has a stack of books in progress simultaneously
- Dislikes gamified reading apps that reduce reading to statistics
- Values their own interpretations over algorithmic recommendations

**Secondary reader:**
- In a book club — uses Shadow Scribe for discussion prep
- Rereads books — wants to track how their reading has changed
- Reads EPUB/audiobooks — needs format-agnostic tracking

**Reader who is NOT the target:**
- Someone who wants to be told what to read next
- Someone who wants social reading with friends
- Someone who wants a reading goal tracker
- Someone primarily motivated by reading streaks

---

## Competitive Positioning

| Product | What it does | What it doesn't do |
|---------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Goodreads** | Social reading log, ratings, recommendations | Deep per-book interpretation, privacy |
| **Readwise** | Surfaces highlights from Kindle/Kobo | Notes you write yourself, spoiler safety |
| **StoryGraph** | Reading statistics, mood-based recs | Companion presence, interpretation tools |
| **Obsidian** | General PKM, highly customizable | Opinionated reading UX, literary voice |
| **Paper journal** | Fully personal, tactile | Searchable, structured, always available |
| **Shadow Scribe** | Private, literary, interpretive companion | Recommendations, social features, highlights sync |

Shadow Scribe's differentiator: **the companion that speaks back**. The presence engine (`generatePresence`) and the literary voice set it apart from every tracker-class product.

---

## The Promise

> *Shadow Scribe keeps what matters from a book — and says something true about the experience of reading it.*

This is the product promise. It has three parts:

1. **Keeps what matters** — notes, characters, mysteries, progress, reflections — all in one place, privately owned
2. **From a book** — not from the internet, not from the author, not from other readers
3. **Says something true** — the presence engine; the companion insights; the literary voice

| Readwise | Productive, system-focused, spaced-repetition |

No other product in this category speaks the way Shadow Scribe speaks. This is a moat.

---

## What Must Never Change

Regardless of how the product grows, these must remain stable:

1. **Private by default.** No social features, no public profiles, no sharing unless the reader explicitly initiates it (and even then, read-only).
2. **The reader owns their data.** JSON export, print view, localStorage — the reader can always leave with everything.
3. **The voice.** The literary tone is not a design choice — it is the product's identity.
4. **Spoiler safety.** The companion never tells the reader something they haven't read yet.
5. **No gamification.** No streaks as rewards, no badges, no points, no daily goals.

## Positioning Statement (Internal)

Shadow Scribe is a **private reading companion** for serious readers who believe that the experience of reading a book deserves more than a star rating. It holds your notes, your mysteries, your characters, and your interpretations — and speaks back to you in a voice that feels earned by the act of reading itself.
