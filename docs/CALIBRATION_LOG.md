# Lantern — Alpha Calibration Log
**Started:** 2026-05-26 · Alpha Week 1

This document is the living record of real-reader signals during the public alpha.
It is not a feature backlog. It is not a bug tracker. It is an observation record.

The question throughout is not: *what should we build?*
The question is: *where does Lantern feel real, and where does it feel designed?*

---

## How to Use This Log

Add entries when you observe:
- A reader hesitating or abandoning a flow
- A reader misunderstanding the companion
- A reader feeling emotionally engaged
- A reader expecting chat behavior
- Silence that felt broken rather than intentional
- A phrase that felt performed rather than observed
- A moment of genuine surprise or delight

Each entry needs: what happened, where it happened, what it might mean.
Avoid prescribing solutions. Observe first.

## Onboarding Signals — What to Watch For

**EPUB import:**
- Readers who abandon mid-import (what step?)
- Readers confused by "pattern matching only" vs. AI extraction
- Readers who don't understand what chapter structure is used for

**Manual companion creation:**
- Readers who skip the ISBN field (cover art matters)
- Readers who don't understand "Companion Mood"
- Readers who are confused by the chapter number step

**First note:**
- How long before a first note is left?
- Do readers understand the tag system?
- Does the companion thread response land or feel strange?

**API key:**
- Do readers find the Settings path intuitive?
- Does "Not set" feel alarming or benign?

---

## Trust + Persistence Signals — What to Watch For

- Readers asking "where is my data?"
- Readers who export immediately (trust anxiety)
- Readers who don't export at all (false confidence or genuine trust)
- Readers confused by "your data never leaves this device"
- Readers asking about backup/sync

---

## Silence Signals — What to Watch For

Silence should feel intentional, not broken. Watch for:
- Readers thinking the companion is malfunctioning (expecting chat)
- Readers who never see a companion observation (too little)
- Readers who find companion intrusive (too much)
- Readers who describe the companion as "different" or "strange" (good)
- Readers who describe it as "quiet" or "alive" (exactly right)

## What Not to Build

Things that have been explicitly considered and set aside during this phase.
This list is a commitment, not a failure list.

- Social features (sharing, following, public libraries)
- Recommendation engine ("readers like you enjoyed…")
- Achievement / streak gamification (beyond the quiet session counter)
- Reading goals / habit tracking
- Dashboard analytics for the reader
- AI chat interface ("ask the companion anything")
- Highlights import from Kindle / other sources
- Cloud sync (architecture is ready; not yet needed)
- Community features of any kind
